Mountain Biking

Mountain biking is an exciting way to explore rugged terrain and take in the beauty of the mountains. As you ride along winding trails, you feel the cool mountain air, hear the crunch of gravel beneath your tires, and see sweeping views that make every climb worth it. Whether you’re tackling steep paths or cruising through forested routes, mountain biking offers a thrilling mix of adventure and nature. Warm summer days and early fall weather make the experience even better, giving you clear skies and comfortable temperatures.

Best mountains to visit for mountain biking

  • Moab, Utah
  • Bentonville, Arkansas
  • Pisgah National Forest, North Carolina

All these places have locations where you can rent bikes and enjoy.
