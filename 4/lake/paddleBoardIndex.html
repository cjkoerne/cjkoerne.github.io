Paddleboarding

Paddleboarding is a great way to get into nature. You get to glide across the water and admire the trees and mountains that surround the water. Warm summer days make paddleboarding a great activity because there often times is a breeze on the water.

Best Lakes to visit for paddleboarding

  • Lake Tahoe, California/Nevada
  • Lake Powell, Utah/Nevada
  • Grand Tenton National Park, Wyoming

Paddleboarding . If you catch Lake Tahoe on a sunny day, you will find that the water is very clear and you can see the rocks and lakebed. Lake Powell offers views that can not be found anywhere else in the world. The cayons around reflect the rocks orange and red tones. Grand Tenton National Park offers a range of lakes within a close proximity. These lakes are surrounded by mountains for an jaw dropping view.
