Water Skiing

Water skiing gives a thrill. You are being pulled across the water at high speeds. The best lake for this is ones that are large and have a lot of calm water. The calm water allows for a better experience on the skiis.

Best Lakes to visit for water skiing

  • Tappan Lake, Ohio
  • Lake Travis, Texas
  • Lake Placid, New York

Water skiing is perfect for a thrill seeker. Lake Havasu’s warm desert climate keeps the water comfortable even outside of summer, making it a top spot for skiers. Lake Travis is known for its long stretches of calm water, giving skiers plenty of room to practice tricks or simply enjoy the ride. Lake Placid offers a completely different experience with its colder mountain air and beautiful scenery.
