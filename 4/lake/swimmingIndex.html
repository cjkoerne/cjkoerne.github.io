Swimming

Swimming in the ocean is one of the most favored activities at the beach. On a hot summer day, the salty waves wash over your skin, cooling you down as the warm sun shines above. The sound of crashing waves fills the air while the ocean breeze carries the fresh, salty scent of the sea. Dive beneath the sparkling water, feel the cool current rush past you, and let the waves carry you along. It allows you to go back to work feeling refreshed.

Best Beaches to visit for swimming

  • Hawaii- Warm all year and has a tropical vibe. The water is clear and warm. There is good waves to enjoy and even wildlife in the water
  • California- The Pacific coast includes colder water.
  • Florida: White sandy beaches with famously warm water from the Atlantic Ocean. The sun is always shining.

All of these locations are in the United States and very spread out. California is on the West coast, while Florida is in the South. Hawaii is on its own island in the middle of the pacific. The Northeast of the United States also has beaches, but for some, the weather at them is less than ideal. These places are good to visit year round as they are warm all the time. They are all fairly close to the equator, which gives them good sunny weather most days of the year.
