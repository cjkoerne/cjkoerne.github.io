The Beach

The beach is a great way to get outdoors. Depending on which beach you go to, you can experience sunny weather, while others are cloudy all year round. The some common activities include Swimming, Parasailing, Snorkeling, and Sand Games. These are all best to do at a beach where the sun is shining and the sand is soft. In the United States, States like Florida, Hawaii, and California are all notorious Beach spots as they are warm year round and not only for the summer. Many beaches in the northern parts of the states have colder beaches, but offer what they can in the summer time when the sun is shining.
