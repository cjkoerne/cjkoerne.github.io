Sand Games

Sand games are the perfect beach activity for anyone who wants to have fun without getting in the water. Build towering sandcastles, challenge your friends to a game of volleyball or spikeball, or simply enjoy the warm sand beneath your feet. With the sound of waves crashing nearby and the sun shining overhead, you can enjoy all the excitement of the beach while staying safely on shore. It’s a great choice for people who don’t know how to swim, are nervous around the water, or simply prefer to keep their feet in the sand!

Best Beaches to visit for sand games

  • Rehoboth Beach, Delaware — A quieter beach that has soft sand and allows for playing in the sand peacefully.
  • Huntington Beach, California — This beach has a whole community of beach volleyball players. The weather is always nice in Southern California!
  • Clearwater Beach, Florida — Another family beach that includes soft white sand that encourages young ones to play in!

These beaches all offer their own unique type of sand, creating different experiences depending on where you go. Rehoboth Beach, Delaware has soft, fine, tan colored sand that is easy to mold and perfect for sandcastles. Huntington Beach, California offeres a coarser golden sand that stays firm under your feet, which make it perfect for beach sports like volleyball. Clearwater Beach, Florida is known across the world for its powder soft, bright white sand that stays relievely cool, even on a hot day, which allows for a comfortable experience playing in it. Each places sand adds to the experience.
