Snorkeling

Snorkeling is the perfect beach activity for animal lovers who want to see marine life from a whole new perspective. Swim alongside colorful fish, watch sea turtles glide through the water, and discover the fascinating world beneath the waves. It’s almost like visiting an aquarium, except you’re right there in the water with the animals in their natural home. Put on your mask, take a breath, and get ready for an unforgettable underwater adventure!

Best Beaches to visit for snorkeling

  • Florida Keys, Florida
  • Hawaii
  • Virgin Islands

Snorkeling is one of the best way to explore the ocean up close, and each these destinations offer its own unique underwater experience. In Key West, Florida, the clear water and calm waves allow you to experience the vibrant coral reefs and tropical fish. In Hawaii, the water stays warm and you can catch a school of tropical fish, or even a sea turtle. The Caribbean Seas in the Virgin Islands offer colorful sights of starfish, sting rays, and beautiful coral.
