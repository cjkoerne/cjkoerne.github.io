Bird Watching

Bird watching in the desert is surprisingly peaceful and full of life. Colorful birds perched on cacti, cliffs, and desert shrubs. Many desert birds have unique calls that echo across the open landscape. Whether you’re watching hummingbirds, hawks, or roadrunners, the desert offers a calm and beautiful environment for observing wildlife.

Best deserts to visit for bird watching

  • Saguaro National Park, Arizona
  • Big Bend National Park, Texa
  • Anza‑Borrego Desert, California

There are so many different types of birds. They have nice calls and beautiful colors.
