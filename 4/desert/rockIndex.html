Rock Climbing

Rock climbing in the desert offers dramatic cliffs, warm sunshine, and breathtaking views from every angle. The desert’s unique rock formations create perfect climbing routes for beginners and experts alike. As you climb, you can feel the heat radiating from the rocks and see the vast desert stretching out below you. The quiet environment makes every climb feel peaceful, focused, and rewarding.

Best deserts to visit for rock climbing

  • Red Rock Canyon, Nevada
  • Joshua Tree National Park, California
  • Smith Rock State Park, Orego

These places have amazing rock that would be amazing to visit.
