ATV

ATV riding in the desert is a thrilling way to explore by rolling through sand dunes. As you speed across the warm desert ground, you can feel the hot desert sun and wind rushing past your face. The terrain changes constantly from soft sand to rocky paths giving every ride a mix of excitement and challenge. Desert ATV trails often stretch for miles, letting you enjoy the scenery..

Best deserts to visit for ATV

  • Glamis Sand Dunes, California
  • Moab, Utah
  • Imperial Sand Dunes, Arizona

The desert is an amazing place to zoom around in an ATV. These places have large open areas so you can drive wild.
